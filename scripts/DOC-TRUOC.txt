========================================
  1900 RETROFOTO - CAI DAT
========================================

BUOC 1: Chuot phai vao file  CAI-DAT.bat
        chon "Run as administrator"

        (Bat buoc phai chon dong nay.
         Nhap dup binh thuong se bao loi.)

BUOC 2: Dien 4 o hien ra:

        - Mat khau nhan vien: tu dat, tu 4 ky tu
        - Thu muc luu anh:    doi sang o con nhieu
                              cho trong, vd E:\photobooth
        - Thu muc may anh:    doi giong o tren, vd E:\Anh
        - Cong:               de nguyen 8090

BUOC 3: Bam nut "Cai dat"

        Xong. Server chay luon, va tu chay
        moi lan bat may.

        CHUP MAN HINH bang dia chi hien ra.

        Trinh cai dat da TU LAM gium:
        tat che do ngu, mo firewall, dang ky
        tu chay lai khi server chet.


========================================
  CON 1 VIEC NEN LAM
========================================

GHIM DIA CHI CO DINH

   Nhap dup loi tat  DAT-IP-TINH
   (ngoai man hinh). Bam Yes, roi go chu
   g  va Enter.

   Khong phai dien so nao ca - no doc lai
   dung bo so may dang chay tot roi ghim lai.
   Neu ghim xong ma mat mang, no tu tra lai
   nhu cu ngay.

   VI SAO: ma QR co chua dia chi may chu.
   Khong ghim thi mat dien la dia chi doi,
   va moi ma QR da phat cho khach se HONG.


========================================
  DUNG HANG NGAY
========================================

Mo trang quan ly:
   Nhap dup loi tat  1900 Retrofoto
   (ngoai man hinh)

Xem con bao nhieu cho trong:
   Goc phai tren trang quan ly co den bao
   dung luong. Vang la nen don, do la don
   NGAY (bam vao den -> bam moc don dep).


========================================
  CO VAN DE? CHI CAN 3 NUT
========================================

1. KHONG BIET DANG BI GI
   -> Nhap dup  KIEM-TRA  (ngoai man hinh)

   No tu kiem tra het roi in ra DUNG VIEC
   CAN LAM. Cu lam theo danh sach do.
   Day la nut nen bam DAU TIEN, luon luon.

2. TRANG KHONG LEN / SERVER CHET
   -> Nhap dup  KHOI-DONG-LAI  (ngoai man hinh)
      Cho khoang 20 giay.

   Thuong thi khong can lam gi: he thong tu
   kiem tra 5 phut mot lan va tu bat lai.

3. DIA CHI BI DOI (sau mat dien, doi WiFi)
   -> Nhap dup loi tat  SUA-IP  (ngoai man hinh)

   No tu tim dia chi moi, sua cau hinh, bat
   lai server, va ghi danh sach dia chi moi
   ra file DIA-CHI-1900RETROFOTO.txt ngoai
   man hinh.

   LUU Y: loi tat tren TUNG MAY TRONG PHONG
   CHUP van ghi dia chi cu -> phai ra tung
   phong sua bang tay theo file do.


========================================
  MAY LOI HAY GAP
========================================

"Can chay bang quyen Administrator"
   -> Chua chuot phai chon Run as administrator.
      Lam lai buoc 1.

Dien thoai khach khong quet duoc QR
   -> Dien thoai phai noi WIFI CUA QUAN.
      Dung 4G se khong vao duoc.
   -> Van khong duoc: nhap dup KIEM-TRA.

Trang trang, khong hien gi
   -> Nhan Ctrl + Shift + R trong trinh duyet.

Cong bi chiem
   -> Chay lai CAI-DAT.bat, doi Cong thanh 8095.

Bam "Da chup xong" ma khong thay anh
   -> Anh phai nam trong thu muc ten dung
      bang 4 so cua ma, vd E:\Anh\8206\

Chi tiet hon: mo file HUONG-DAN.md


========================================
  VIEC NEN LAM HANG TUAN
========================================

1. Chep thu muc anh (vd E:\photobooth) sang
   o cung ngoai. O hong la mat sach.

2. Nhap dup KIEM-TRA mot lan. Mat 15 giay,
   va no phat hien duoc nhung thu dang am
   tham sai ma chua ai thay.


========================================
  GO BO KHOI MAY
========================================

Chuot phai vao  GO-CAI-DAT.bat
chon "Run as administrator", go chu g
va Enter.

No tu dung server, bo phan tu chay moi
lan bat may, dong firewall, tra lai che
do ngu va dia chi mang nhu cu.

ANH KHACH KHONG BI XOA. Script in ra
duong dan thu muc anh de ban tu chep di
noi khac truoc khi xoa.

Sau do vao Settings > Apps go not
"1900 Retrofoto" khoi may.
